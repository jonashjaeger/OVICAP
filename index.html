MODEL DOCUMENTATION

An estimate is not an observation.

OVICAP estimates species-specific NISP among osteologically ambiguous sheep/goat remains. It deliberately does not estimate MNI, herd size, domestication intensity, or living population numbers.

01 · Accounting

Each ambiguous specimen belongs to the original Ovis/Capra NISP. Identified ZooMS specimens are reallocated from that pool, not added a second time. Inconclusive specimens remain in the unresolved pool.

Nunresolved = Nambiguous − NZooMS sheep − NZooMS goat − NZooMS other

02 · Bayesian model

Under three-taxon mode, the proportions of sheep, goat and other in the ambiguous pool follow a Dirichlet prior with the same editable concentration for each category. Diagnostic ZooMS identifications update it. Under two-taxon mode, goat vs sheep uses the corresponding Beta model, while known other taxa remain excluded.

OVICAP generates 6,000 posterior-predictive assignments of all unresolved fragments. It reports predictive means and central 95% intervals. The model assumes ZooMS taxon-specific identification success does not bias the retained outcomes, and that the selection design is exchangeable with unresolved fragments.

03 · Detection planning

P(≥1 positive) = 1 − (1 − p × q)n

Here p is a hypothetical rare-species proportion, q is the probability of a diagnostic ZooMS result, and n is the number of attempted analyses. The finite-population option uses an exact hypergeometric no-detection probability, without replacement, and assumes all chosen specimens are identifiable.

04 · Caveats

A purposively selected group of mandibular teeth may not be representative of a whole fragmented assemblage. Taxon, element, context, collagen survival, chronology, and preservation can all affect apparent proportions. Report observed NISP and modelled NISP separately, ideally using stratified sampling when possible.

For scarce species, zero detected bones is not evidence that the species was entirely absent. Prior choice strongly affects intervals when the diagnostic sample is small or dominated by one species.
